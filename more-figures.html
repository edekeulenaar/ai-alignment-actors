      GroupCategoryAlso known asDefinition and decision ruleData points to code
      A. Self-governance (normative)Organizational principles, standards, codesAI principles, charters, codes of conduct, responsible AI standards, human rights statementsBinds the organization. States values or requirements for how the company develops and deploys AI in general, not for one model or risk domain.Version; date; scope (whole firm / division); enforcement mechanism named?
      Model behavior specificationsConstitutions, model specs, character documentsWritten target for model behavior, used in training or evaluation. Addressed to the model and to users.Version; date; priority ordering of values; stated training use
      Frontier governance frameworksResponsible Scaling Policy, Frontier Safety Framework, Preparedness Framework, Frontier Governance Framework, Frontier AI Framework, SB 53 compliance frameworkDefines capability or risk thresholds, the evaluations used to detect them, and the safeguards or deployment decisions that follow. Decision rule: the document commits the company to if-then actions tied to thresholds.Version; date; risk domains; threshold definitions; named evaluators; legal basis (voluntary / SB 53 / EU GPAI Code)
      Usage policies and termsAcceptable use policy, usage policy, consumer terms, use guidelinesRules for users and deployers. Decision rule: the addressee is the user or customer.Version; date; prohibited-use list; enforcement stated
      B. DisclosureModel documentationSystem cards, model cards, addenda, technical reports, training-data summaries, application cardsDocuments one model, model family, or product: training, capabilities, evaluations, limitations.Model; release date; sections present; third-party evaluators named; legal template used (e.g., EU AI Act training-content summary)
      Framework implementation reportsFrontier Safety Framework reports, risk reports, alignment risk updates, ASL activations, framework update logsApplies a named frontier framework to a model or moment. Decision rule: it cites a framework threshold and reports a determination.Framework and version cited; thresholds reached (Y/N); safeguards triggered
      Misuse and threat intelligence reportsThreat reports, disruption reportsReports observed misuse of the company's systems by identified or unidentified actors and the enforcement taken.Period covered; actor types; countries; harm domains; enforcement actions
      Transparency and compliance reportsResponsible AI transparency reports, voluntary commitments trackers, regulatory compliance disclosuresPeriodic or standing account of the company's governance practice as a whole, often keyed to commitments or regulation.Reporting period; commitments or laws referenced; metrics disclosed
      Certifications and third-party assessmentsAudits, certifications, external testing, cross-lab evaluationsAssessment performed or certified by a party other than the company. Decision rule: an external party issues or conducts it.Assessor; standard (ISO/IEC 42001, SOC 2); scope; date
      Statements and incident disclosuresPublic statements, postmortems, incident reportsResponds to a specific event: a government action, a dispute, an incident. Decision rule: dated to and triggered by an event.Triggering event; counterparty; date; actions announced
      C. External governancePublic policy positionsPolicy submissions, regulatory comments, legislative endorsements, blueprintsAddressed to governments or policy communities; states what law or public policy should require. This is what most of anthropic.com/policy contains, but see Data issues sheet.Addressee (institution, jurisdiction); bill or docket; position; date
      Commitments (unilateral and multilateral)Voluntary commitments, pledges, codes of practice signatures, industry principlesCompany undertakes a future obligation, alone or with others. Decision rule: explicit 'we commit / we sign'.Unilateral or multilateral; co-signatories; convenor; binding status
      Partnerships and MOUsPartnerships, memoranda of understanding, government collaborationsBilateral or multilateral agreement with a named external party around a project or issue.Counterparty type (state, NGO, firm, AISI); country; sector; instrument (MOU, contract)
      Governance bodies and structuresTrusts, advisory councils, expert councils, institutes, industry bodiesCreates or describes a standing body that shapes company decisions or sector coordination.Body type; members; formal powers; internal or inter-firm
      D. Programmes and knowledgeProgrammes and fundingInitiatives, grants, fellowships, bug bounties, access programmesCompany-run programme that funds, invites, or grants access to external actors.Amount; recipients; eligibility; duration
      Safeguards and product-safety explainersSafety approach posts, safeguards announcementsDescribes a mitigation, safeguard, or product-safety measure the company has built or applied.Harm domain; user group (e.g., teens); mechanism type (classifier, policy, UX)
      ResearchPapers, preprints, research blog postsAdvances knowledge on an AI issue; typically has authors and methods.Authors; venue; method; alignment topic
      Evaluations, benchmarks and toolsBenchmarks, eval suites, open-source auditing toolsReleases a reusable instrument for measuring or auditing models.What is measured; open or closed; licence; adoption by others
      Essays and vision statementsLeadership essays, vision postsLeadership articulates views on AI's future, ethics, or societal role, without committing to specific actions.Author; date; themes
